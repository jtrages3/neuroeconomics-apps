The Ultimatum Game

How the game works

Each round, a partner has $10 to split with you. They propose how much they keep and how much you get. You decide:

  • Accept — the money is split as proposed.
  • Reject — neither of you gets anything.

You will play 20 rounds: once with each of 10 people and 10 rounds with a computer, in random order. Each game with a person is a single, one-time interaction — your decision will not be shown to the other partners and cannot affect their offers.

Meet your partners

You'll play one round with each of these people.

Ready?

The game takes about 3–4 minutes.

Respond with the on-screen buttons, or press F to accept and J to reject.
