Part 1 — Explore
Three things to try
Task A. Set the ticket price to $50, the prize to $100, and the probability to 50%. What does Pascal's rule say? Does this feel right to you? Why or why not?
Task B. Find a combination of price, prize, and probability where the EV of the ticket is positive but you personally would not buy it. Describe the numbers you chose and explain why you would pass.
Task C. Pascal's model ignores your current wealth and your feelings about risk. Based on what you observed in the calculator, describe one situation where you think Pascal's rule gives good advice and one situation where it seems to break down.
